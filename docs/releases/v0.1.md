# SwingCheck 0.1
Released October 5, 2026 · v0.1.0 to v0.1.3

The first public release: upload a down-the-line video of one swing, mark the club, and get a check of eight positions through the swing.

## What's new

- **Eight checkpoints, down the line:** address posture, swing plane, takeaway, halfway back, top, downswing, impact, and follow-through. Each is green (good), yellow (watch), or red (fix), with the measurements behind it.
- **Marking:** move the slider to each position and click the ball, the club, and your hands. A magnifier helps you aim, on a phone too.
- **Results:** an annotated video, a key frame per checkpoint with its lines, a summary that names the one thing to work on first, and a text report.
- **Online at swingcheck.org:** no account. Your swings are private to your browser, with a private link to open them on another device. Each visitor keeps up to 4 swings, deleted 3 days after upload.
- **Terms of use and a Privacy notice,** plus support through Ko-fi and InstaPay.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.1.1:** the downswing is green from 10% above the swing plane, so "On the line" is never yellow; the version shows under the name.
- **v0.1.2:** frames load faster while marking, and a new release never mixes old and new files in the browser.
- **v0.1.3:** a welcome heading for first-time visitors, "banking app", and the Oxford comma everywhere.
