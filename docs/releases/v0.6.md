# SwingCheck 0.6
Released October 6, 2026 · v0.6.0 to v0.6.16

Marking, reworked to explain itself, and swings you can rename.

## What's new

- **Two parts to every step:** first slide to match the example and press Frame looks right, then click the points one at a time. The frame stays put while you click, and Change frame goes back.
- **The example where you look:** Rory McIlroy at the same moment, beside your frame on a wide screen, or as a small picture in the corner, placed clear of you.
- **The ball on the takeaway:** the club often hides it at address, and it's in the same spot.
- **A color per point:** the ball is a yellow ring, the club pink, and your hands a blue square.
- **Messages where you are:** save errors show right under the frame.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.6.1:** rename a swing with the pencil after its name. A shared PDF follows the new name.
- **v0.6.2:** on the example, the point to click next pulses.
- **v0.6.3:** Rory's example uses our latest, most accurate marks, with every checkpoint green.
- **v0.6.4 to v0.6.6:** Rory's takeaway example is on an earlier frame, with the clubhead clearly marked on the club, and a new example picture always replaces the old one.
- **v0.6.7:** on Rory's halfway back, the hands box sits on his hands.
- **v0.6.8:** a bigger, clearer frame slider, and "Drag the slider to match the example".
- **v0.6.9:** driver swings get their own example, Tiger Woods with a driver, and black bars around a video are cropped off.
- **v0.6.10:** key frames open large on the page instead of a new tab, a clearer "Your swings" button, and the slo-mo tip in plain words.
- **v0.6.11:** Open link beside Copy link, to see your shared PDF as others do.
- **v0.6.12 to v0.6.14:** an anonymous count of how long marking takes, to guide what to make easier next.
- **v0.6.15:** a share link that was stopped, or whose swing was deleted, opens a page saying so, with a way to check your own swing.
- **v0.6.16:** when the server is nearly full, new uploads pause with a message until space frees up, and key frames take less space.
