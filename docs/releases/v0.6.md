# SwingCheck 0.6
Released October 6, 2026 · v0.6.0 to v0.6.4

Marking, reworked to explain itself, and swings you can rename.

## What's new

- **Two parts to every step:** first slide to match the example and press Frame looks right, then click the points one at a time. The frame stays put while you click, and Change frame goes back.
- **The example where you look:** Rory McIlroy at the same moment, beside your frame on a wide screen, or as a small picture in the corner, placed clear of you.
- **The ball on the takeaway:** the club often hides it at address, and it's in the same spot.
- **A color per point:** the ball is a yellow ring, the club pink, and your hands a blue square.
- **Messages where you are:** save errors show right under the frame.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.6.1:** rename a swing with the pencil after its name. A shared PDF follows the new name.
- **v0.6.2:** on the example, the point to click next pulses.
- **v0.6.3:** Rory's example uses our latest, most accurate marks, with every checkpoint green.
- **v0.6.4:** on Rory's takeaway, the clubhead mark sits on the club, not his hip.
