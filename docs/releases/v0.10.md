# SwingCheck 0.10
Released October 27, 2026 · v0.10.0 to v0.10.2

Face-on: your swing from the front.

## What's new

- **Face-on videos:** film from in front of you and choose Face-on when you upload. It shows what down-the-line can't: sway, weight shift, your head, and whether your hands lead the club at impact.
- **Five face-on checkpoints:** Address (stance width, ball position, spine tilt), Top (sway, head), Downswing (hips leading, head back), Impact (hands ahead of the ball, hips forward, head behind the ball), and Finish (weight on the front side).
- **Quick to mark:** click the ball at address and your hands at impact; the other steps are just picking the frame. The examples are Tiger Woods with an iron and Rory McIlroy with a driver.
- **A face-on camera check and setup guide:** the camera straight in front of you at hip height, lined up with the middle of your stance.
- **Checked on real swings:** six tour pros read green on every face-on checkpoint.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.10.0:** the support card says tips help keep SwingCheck online and free.
- **v0.10.1:** face-on drawings show how far your head and hips moved since address: a line where they started, an arrow to where they are, and the distance in words.
- **v0.10.2:** a new face-on iron example of Tiger Woods, filmed with a still camera.
