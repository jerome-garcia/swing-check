# SwingCheck 0.5
Released October 6, 2026 · v0.5.0 to v0.5.6

Share your results, a camera setup guide, clearer errors, fairer ranges, and a friendlier summary.

## What's new

- **Download PDF and Share PDF:** sharing makes a link that opens your summary PDF, with a preview card in chats. It follows your latest results and ends when the swing is deleted, or sooner with Stop sharing.
- **How to set up the camera:** down-the-line and face-on setup pictures from OnForm (used with permission), on the upload page and on the home page before your first swing.
- **Clearer marking:** marks that can't be right, like a frame out of order or clicks swapped, stop Save and analyze and say what to fix.
- **Fairer ranges:** the takeaway is green up to 20 cm toward the ball, and the follow-through allows a flatter exit, as good players often have.
- **A friendlier summary:** "Tour-level swing" when every checkpoint is green, a green "Good swing" note when nothing is red, and "What to try" instead of "How to fix" for yellow checkpoints.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.5.2:** an impact frame set before the top gives a plain error that says what to do.
- **v0.5.3:** a down-the-line swing uses the top you marked, and swing-detection errors are in plain words.
- **v0.5.6:** "Every checkpoint is green, from address to follow-through. Keep swinging just like this."
