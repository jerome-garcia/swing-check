# SwingCheck 0.3
Released October 5, 2026 · v0.3.0 to v0.3.3

Better suggested frames, a fairer swing plane, and much faster pictures.

## What's new

- **Better suggested frames while marking:** the takeaway is found from how far your hands have travelled, and halfway back from how far they have risen. Both land within about a frame on our test clips.
- **The swing plane points at your belt buckle:** the line from the club neck toward the belt, so a slightly upright or flat setup is judged fairly.
- **Faster results pages:** key frames load as small pictures (about 120 KB instead of 6.5 MB), and the annotated video is about three times smaller. The results don't change.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.3.1:** the address step always opens on the first frame, and the note about suggested frames sits above the instruction bar.
- **v0.3.2:** "Deletes on Oct 8" instead of "Deleted Oct 8".
