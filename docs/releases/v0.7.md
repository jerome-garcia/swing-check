# SwingCheck 0.7
Released October 7, 2026 · v0.7.0

Easier marking on a phone.

## What's new

- **Drag a point to move it:** a point that landed a little off can be dragged to where it belongs, with the magnifier showing as you move it. No more Undo and trying again.
- **Pinch to zoom:** on a phone, pinch the frame to zoom in for a closer look at the ball, club, and hands, and pinch out to zoom back.
