# SwingCheck 0.8
Released October 10, 2026 · v0.8.0 to v0.8.2

Tell us what you think.

## What's new

- **Send feedback:** rate SwingCheck from 1 to 5 and add a message if you like, from the Send feedback line under the tip message, on every page. No name or email needed.
- **Questions go to the Feedback page:** the Terms of use and Privacy notice now point there instead of Ko-fi.
- **Privacy notice:** says what feedback keeps (your rating, message, and when it was sent) and that it's kept until deleted.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.8.1:** the footer's Feedback link is gone; Send feedback in the support card covers it.
- **v0.8.2:** the light and dark mode link in the footer is now a small switch, with a moon or a sun that slides across.
