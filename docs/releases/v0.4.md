# SwingCheck 0.4
Released October 6, 2026 · v0.4.0 to v0.4.2

A club choice per swing, and fewer false alarms on things good players do.

## What's new

- **Iron or wedge, or driver or wood:** pick it when you upload, or change it later. A driver gets its own ranges: spine bend 25° to 40°, arms up to 20° out, and a flatter shaft at 35° to 55°.
- **Watch, not Fix:** the shaft angle, hands behind your back heel at the top, and the follow-through can be yellow at most. They're worth a look, but good players differ there.

## Fixes and tweaks

- **v0.4.1:** after a re-analysis, the key frames always show the new results.
- **v0.4.2:** watch-only rows say so the same way: "Fix: none (a watch item only)."
