# SwingCheck 0.11
Released October 27, 2026 · v0.11.0

A still camera, checked.

## What's new

- **The camera check spots a moving camera:** if the camera pans, zooms, or is handheld during the clip, the camera check says so right after upload, for both views. SwingCheck measures your body against the picture, so a moving camera reads as you moving and gives wrong results.
- **Only clear movement counts:** the club, a passer-by, or a single jumpy frame doesn't set it off. Every still-camera clip we tested passed.
